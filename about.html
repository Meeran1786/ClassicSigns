About Classic Signs

A workshop in Malakpet that has spent over two decades helping Hyderabad's businesses and families get noticed.

Our story

Craftsmanship you can see from the street

Classic Signs started in 2000 with a simple goal: make signs that look great and keep working for years. Today we make everything from LED shop signboards and back-lit boards to custom neon, infinity mirror letters, stainless steel lettering and Islamic calligraphy.

Every sign is designed with the customer, built in our own workshop and installed by our team. That's how we keep quality high and prices fair.

  • LED signboards and back-lit boards
  • Custom neon for shops, homes and events
  • 3D, infinity mirror and steel letters
  • Nameplates and metal calligraphy
